Our Company

Committed to Clean Water Solutions

Shriram Infra and Tech Services LLP is a dedicated water and wastewater treatment company focused on delivering sewage treatment plants, effluent treatment plants, ZLD systems, and package plants across India.

We are committed to innovative, efficient, and sustainable treatment solutions — serving municipalities, industrial clients, and government bodies with plants that are reliable, compliant, and built to last.

Our approach covers the full project lifecycle: from design and engineering to construction, commissioning, and ongoing O&M — with complete ownership at every stage.
